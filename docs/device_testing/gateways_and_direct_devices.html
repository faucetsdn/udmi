UDMI test results for gateways and direct devices

Test results taken for UDMI devices configured either as direct devices (making an MQTT connection and communicating for themselves) or as UDMI gateways (making an MQTT connection and communicating for other devices). See also the results for proxied devices.

Tests are grouped by bucket. Except for those in the manual bucket, they are sequencer tests and are shown with their feature stage. Select a device at the top of the table for its details and notes.
